# Ambiguity Register — Scenario B: Motor Insurance Underwriting (`AsuransiNusantara`)

> **Disclaimer:** All numerical thresholds, tariff rates, and loading percentages below are **illustrative educational model assumptions** formulated to resolve narrative ambiguities deterministically. They do **not** represent official Indonesian OJK motor insurance tariffs or commercial actuarial pricing standards.

| ID | Original Ambiguous Statement | Open Question | Selected Interpretation | Explicit Threshold / Business Rule | Business Justification | Effect on Decision Model |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AMB-01** | *"Young drivers ... are riskier, so they pay more."* | What age defines a "young driver", what is the minimum insurable age, and how are senior drivers treated? | Three licensed driver bands plus explicit underage/missing guards. | Minimum legal driving age: `< 17` years → `INELIGIBLE` (consistent with Indonesian SIM A/C minimum age 17). Young driver: `[17..24]` years → `HIGH` risk (+25% loading). Prime adult: `[25..65]` years → `LOW` risk (+0%). Senior driver: `> 65` years → `MEDIUM` risk (+10%). Missing DOB → `UNKNOWN`. | Aligns with standard underwriting age-curve risk (inexperience under 25, gradual reaction-time elevation over 65) and avoids illegal policy issuance to unlicensed minors. | `BKM01` computes integer completed years; `D01` classifies `LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/INELIGIBLE/UNKNOWN`; feeds `D05` and `D08`. |
| **AMB-02** | *"those with recent claims are riskier, so they pay more."* | How far back is "recent", and how many claims separate moderate vs. severe claims risk? | 24-month rolling lookback window up to `evaluationDate`, stratified by count. | Recent window: `claimDate >= (evaluationDate - P24M)` and `claimDate <= evaluationDate` (24 months inclusive of exact 2-year anniversary). `0` recent claims → `LOW` (+0%); `1` recent claim → `MEDIUM` (+20%); `>= 2` recent claims → `HIGH` (+40%, triggers referral). | A 24-month horizon captures current driving behavior without permanently penalizing isolated legacy incidents; 2+ claims within 2 years signals loss-frequency instability requiring senior review. | `BKM02` counts recent claims using calendar duration `P24M`; `D02` outputs `LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/UNKNOWN`. |
| **AMB-03** | *"missing claims history"* (explicitly flagged in scenario) | Should unavailable claims records be treated as zero claims (`[]`) or flagged for review? | Strict distinction between verified zero claims and missing/unverifiable history. | `claimsHistoryAvailable != true` (i.e., `false` or `null`) OR `claimDates = null` → `D02 = "UNKNOWN"`, which mandates `D05 = "REFER"` and applies a provisional `+25%` uncertainty loading in `D08`. | Treating missing history as a clean record introduces severe adverse selection and moral hazard; referral allows underwriters to obtain No-Claim Bonus (NCB) or clearinghouse proof. | `claimsHistoryAvailable` boolean input added alongside `claimDates`; `D02` includes explicit `UNKNOWN` state; `D05` refers `UNKNOWN`. |
| **AMB-04** | *"Very old vehicles are hard to insure — beyond some age we just decline."* | At what vehicle age does eligibility end, and does aging affect risk before decline? | Maximum insurable vehicle age of 15 completed years, with an intermediate aging tier. | `vehicleAge > 15` (i.e., `>= 16` completed years from `vehicleManufactureDate` to `evaluationDate`) → `D03 = "INELIGIBLE"` → `D05 = "DECLINE"`. Vehicles aged `[11..15]` years remain `ELIGIBLE` but elevate `D04` to at least `MEDIUM` (and require inspection if `COMPREHENSIVE`). | Parts obsolescence, safety degradation, and moral hazard increase sharply beyond 15 years; 11–15 year vehicles remain insurable under tighter inspection and risk pricing. | `BKM01` computes `vehicleAge`; `D03` enforces `<= 15` years; `D04` and `D06` apply 10-year aging rule. |
| **AMB-05** | *"Very high vehicle value means an inspection."* | What IDR sum-insured threshold triggers inspection, and does it apply to TPO or declined policies? | Rp500,000,000 threshold applying to all non-declined policies regardless of cover type, plus older comprehensive policies. | If `D05 = "DECLINE"` → `D06 = "NOT_APPLICABLE"`. Else if `insuredValue >= 500,000,000` (IDR) OR (`coverType = "COMPREHENSIVE"` and `vehicleAge > 10`) → `D06 = "REQUIRED"`. Otherwise → `D06 = "NOT_REQUIRED"`. | High-value assets (≥ Rp500M) carry substantial fraud/anti-theft/valuation exposure even on TPO; older vehicles seeking Comprehensive cover need pre-existing damage verification; declined risks never incur inspection cost. | `D06` consumes `D05`, `insuredValue`, `coverType`, and `vehicleAge`. |
| **AMB-06** | *"Sporty/high-performance vehicles attract a loading."* | How is "sporty/high-performance" represented without a vehicle-model database? | Explicit three-tier performance classification input. | `performanceClass` ∈ `{"STANDARD", "SPORT", "HIGH_PERFORMANCE"}`. `STANDARD` → +0% loading; `SPORT` → elevates `D04` to ≥ `MEDIUM` and adds `+15%` loading; `HIGH_PERFORMANCE` → forces `D04 = "HIGH"` and adds `+30%` loading. | Because no external vehicle-spec catalog is supplied, an explicit enumerated input preserves interpretability while distinguishing mild sport trims (`SPORT`) from supercars/superbikes (`HIGH_PERFORMANCE`). | `performanceClass` feeds `D04` (vehicle risk) and `D08` (loading calculation). |
| **AMB-07** | *"Engine size matters somehow, especially for motorcycles."* | Which `engineCC` thresholds apply to motorcycles vs. cars, and how do they affect both risk and base premium? | Separate displacement bands for `MOTORCYCLE` vs. `CAR` across risk (`D04`) and base tariff (`D07`). | **Motorcycle:** `(0..150]` cc (low/commuter), `(150..250]` cc (medium), `> 250` cc (high/big-bike). **Car:** `(0..1500]` cc (low/compact), `(1500..2500]` cc (medium/SUV), `> 2500` cc (high/large). Higher cc tiers increase both `D07` base premium rate and `D04` risk level. | A 300 cc motorcycle is a high-powered sport/touring bike with high loss severity, whereas a 300 cc car engine is non-existent/micro-displacement; separate scales prevent cross-class distortion. | `D04` and `D07` both partition `engineCC` conditionally by `vehicleType`. |
| **AMB-08** | *"Comprehensive cover costs more than third-party-only."* | How are TPO vs. Comprehensive base premiums structured to guarantee this invariant? | Distinct rate schedules and minimum floors per `(vehicleType, engineCC, coverType)`. | For every `(vehicleType, engineCC)` tier, Comprehensive uses a strictly higher percentage rate (`1.8%–3.2%` vs. `0.4%–1.0%`) and strictly higher minimum floor (`Rp300k–Rp2.5M` vs. `Rp150k–Rp850k`) than TPO via `max(minFloor, insuredValue * rate)`. | Guarantees mathematically that `BasePremium(COMPREHENSIVE) > BasePremium(TPO)` for all `insuredValue > 0`, reflecting own-damage/theft coverage in Comprehensive. | `D07` uses 12 mutually exclusive `(vehicleType, engineCC, coverType)` pricing rules + 1 invalid fallback (`0`). |
| **AMB-09** | *"whether loadings stack"* | Do multiple risk loadings stack additively, multiplicatively, or take only the maximum? | **Additive stacking** of independent risk loading percentages. | `TotalLoading = L_driver + L_claims + L_vehicleRisk + L_performance`, implemented via `COLLECT SUM (C+)`; `FinalPremium = BasePremium * (1 + TotalLoading)`. | Additive stacking is transparent, auditable in `C+` decision tables, avoids explosive compounding on multi-factor accepted risks, and matches the prompt's canonical formula. | `D08` (`C+`) sums 4 orthogonal loading dimensions; `D09` applies `D07 * (1 + D08)`. |
| **AMB-10** | *"Some risks are too bad — maybe decline, maybe refer to a senior underwriter; the rules should decide."* | What separates `DECLINE`, `REFER`, and `ACCEPT`, and how is priority enforced? | Strict 3-tier hierarchy (`DECLINE > REFER > ACCEPT`) using `FIRST` hit policy in `D05`. | **DECLINE:** `D03 = INELIGIBLE` OR `D01 = INELIGIBLE` OR (`D01=HIGH` and `D02=HIGH` and `D04=HIGH`). **REFER:** `D01=UNKNOWN` OR `D02=UNKNOWN` OR `D02=HIGH` OR (`D01=HIGH` and `D04=HIGH`) OR (`D01=HIGH` and `D02=MEDIUM`). **ACCEPT:** all remaining eligible combinations. | Enforces hard eligibility gates first, escalates severe/uncertain/compound risks to senior underwriters, and prevents an ineligible vehicle from falling through to `ACCEPT`. | `D05` ordered `FIRST` table; `D09` returns `0` for `DECLINE` and indicative quote for `REFER`. |
| **AMB-11** | Borderline & missing/invalid values | Are threshold boundaries inclusive or exclusive, and what happens to null/negative/future dates? | Explicit interval notation (`[a..b]`, `(a..b]`) and defensive null/invalid rules in every table. | `driverAge = 17` → `HIGH` (accepted age); `24` → `HIGH`, `25` → `LOW`; `vehicleAge = 15` → `ELIGIBLE`, `16` → `INELIGIBLE`; `claimDate = evaluationDate - P24M` → counted as recent; `insuredValue = 500,000,000` → `REQUIRED` inspection; `engineCC = 150 / 250 / 1500 / 2500` → lower band (`<=` upper bound). Future manufacture date or negative/null numeric values → `INELIGIBLE`. | Eliminates off-by-one ambiguity at regulatory and underwriting cutoffs and ensures 100% completeness without silent fall-throughs. | Verified across all 9 decision tables and borderline test cases (`TC-02`, `TC-05`–`TC-08`). |
