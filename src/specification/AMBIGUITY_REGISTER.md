# Ambiguity Register — Scenario B: Motor Insurance Underwriting (`AsuransiNusantara`)

> **Governance Distinction (Legal vs. Educational Policy vs. Technical Choice):**
> 1. **Statutory Legal & Regulatory Basis:**
>    - **UU No. 22 Tahun 2009 tentang Lalu Lintas dan Angkutan Jalan (Pasal 81 ayat (2) huruf a):** Establishes `17` years as the minimum age for `SIM A` (passenger cars) and `SIM C` (standard motorcycles).
>    - **Peraturan Kepolisian Negara Republik Indonesia Nomor 5 Tahun 2021 (*Penerbitan dan Penandaan Surat Izin Mengemudi*, JDIH/Korlantas Polri — Pasal 3 & Pasal 8):** Partitions motorcycle licenses by displacement into **SIM C** (`<= 250 cc`, min age **`17`**), **SIM CI** (`> 250 cc` up to `500 cc`, min age **`18`** + prior SIM C), and **SIM CII** (`> 500 cc`, min age **`19`** + prior SIM CI).
>    - **Scope Boundary / Model Limitation:** While `D01` enforces the baseline `< 17` floor (`UU 22/2009` + `SIM C`), `D01` and `D05` do **not** implement displacement-coupled license validation (`SIM CI` age `18` / `SIM CII` age `19` / 12-month upgrade tenure); `UU 22/2009` alone is insufficient for motorcycles `> 250 cc`, and full licensing compliance is explicitly noted as a model limitation.
> 2. **Business-Policy Assumptions (Educational Scenario):** All claims windows (`24` months), vehicle age limits (`15` years), displacement bands (`cc`), `Rp500M` inspection cutoff, base tariff schedules, and additive loading percentages are illustrative educational assumptions (`KS01`/`KS02`). Real Indonesian OJK motor tariffs (**SEOJK No. 6/SEOJK.05/2017**) classify vehicles by sum-insured value categories and three geographic registration regions (`Wilayah 1–3`) rather than pure engine displacement tiers.
> 3. **Technical DMN Design Choices:** Decomposition into `D01`–`D10`, explicit `evaluationDate` input, hit-policy ordering, `BKM01`/`BKM02` duration functions, and `null`-safe premium semantics (`D10`/`D09`) are engineering choices for deterministic DMN 1.3 modeling.
