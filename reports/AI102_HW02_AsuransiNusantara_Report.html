1. Model Overview & Decision Requirements Diagram (DRD)

We formalize motor underwriting for AsuransiNusantara as a deterministic, two-tiered OMG DMN 1.3 decision model separating structural dependencies (DRD) from executable tabular and FEEL logic (Taylor & Purchase, 2019). From ten explicit inputs (I01–I10, including an explicit evaluationDate to eliminate system-clock non-determinism), the model evaluates nine decisions (D01–D09) supported by two reusable Business Knowledge Models (BKM01, BKM02) and three Knowledge Sources (KS01–KS03). All 35 information requirements, 5 knowledge requirements, and 13 authority requirements form a verified 4-tier directed acyclic graph (DAG) in which every decision receives at least two information requirements (Figure 1).

2. Decision Decomposition Rationale

Rather than collapsing underwriting into a single opaque matrix (5 × 4 × 2 × 3 = 120+ combinations), decomposition isolates orthogonal concerns across four tiers: (1) Independent Risk & Eligibility Assessment (Tier 1) separates driver age risk (D01), claims recency risk (D02), hard vehicle eligibility (D03), technical vehicle risk (D04), and base tariff pricing (D07). Splitting D03 from D04 guarantees that vehicles older than 15 years or with invalid inputs are rejected unconditionally rather than traded off against low driver risk. (2) Disposition & Additive Loading (Tier 2) aggregates D01–D04 in D05 under strict precedence, while D08 sums independent surcharge components. (3) Downstream Gating (Tier 3) wires D05 directly into D06 (Inspection) and D09 (Final Premium) so declined policies yield NOT_APPLICABLE and Rp0. Reusable date logic is centralized in BKM01 and BKM02 to prevent duplicated duration formulas.

4. Decision Table Design, Hit-Policy Rationale & Trace Results

All nine decision tables (79 rules total; Appendix C) guarantee zero silent fall-throughs. D01 (Driver Risk) and D09 (Final Premium) use UNIQUE (U) because their intervals/enums are strictly disjoint. D02–D07 use FIRST (F) to enforce explicit priority ordering: D02 guards missing history before counting claims; D03 short-circuits invalid/old vehicles; D04 resolves overlapping technical risks upward (HIGH > MEDIUM > LOW); D05 enforces DECLINE > REFER > ACCEPT; D06 short-circuits declined risks before inspection triggers; and D07 includes a defensive fallback row. D08 uses COLLECT SUM (C+) across four orthogonal dimensions (4 hits always) to implement:

5. FEEL Expressions, Engine Validation & Defect Resolution

Six distinct FEEL expressions covering all five required categories passed 15 / 15 unit tests and 10 / 10 end-to-end DMN cases in feelin@8.2.2 + dmn-moddle@12.2.1 (0 warnings), with dmn-js@18.1.0 visually verifying all 24 DRD shapes and 53 edges (Table 2; full cases in Appendix D). Probing feelin confirmed why years and months duration(dob, evalDate).years is essential: naive days-and-time arithmetic floor((evalDate - dob).days / 365.25) returns 24 instead of 25 on exact 25th birthday 2001-11-25 (9131 / 365.25 = 24.9993). Validation also caught and fixed three real defects: (1) normative DMN 1.3 namespace requires https:// rather than http:// for dmn-moddle/Camunda compatibility; (2) unparenthesized in ... and ... in FEEL-02 returned null until parenthesized; and (3) IEEE-754 float 200M * 0.018 = 3,599,999.9999999995 caused a Rp1 floor() truncation bug in TC-10, resolved via decimal(..., 0).

6. Known Limitations & Machine-Learning Integration (Session 03)

Limitations: (1) Rates and thresholds are illustrative educational assumptions rather than actuarially calibrated claim-severity tables; (2) performanceClass relies on manual/external vehicle lookup; and (3) standalone desktop Camunda Modeler was not installed locally, so editor verification relied on Camunda's underlying dmn-js@18.1.0 engine and official OMG DMN13.xsd validation. ML Integration (Session 03): Rather than replacing transparent underwriting gates, a calibrated ML claim-propensity score p ∈ [0, 1] (Pratt & Malcolm, 2023; Molnar, 2024) can be integrated cleanly either as an inputData node feeding D05 (referring borderline low-rule-risk cases where p ≥ τrefer) or encapsulated inside a BKM scorecard so deterministic statutory rules (age <17, vehicle >15 yrs) retain absolute override authority.
